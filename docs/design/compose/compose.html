The compose surface - a reply panel over a sharpened keyboard

Two screens, both boards. Every rectangle, band, column and text origin is the number the firmware will use; check.mjs binds this page's K to both board headers name-for-name.

GLYPH RENDERING HERE IS APPROXIMATE. The panel draws 1-bit Cozette 6x13 (board 1) and Spleen 8x16 (board 2); this page draws a browser monospace face stepped at each board's real advance and cell. The metrics are the panel's, the ink inside each cell is not. Board 1 has no committed glyph dump in this repo and inventing one would flatter the design, which is a mistake the settings mock already paid for.
