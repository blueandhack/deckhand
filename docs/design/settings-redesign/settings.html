Settings, redesigned — as shipped

Board 2, 320×480. Every glyph below is the real Spleen bitmap read out of Spleen8x16.h and Spleen12x24.h; every position is a constant parsed from board_es3c35p.h. No Mac font is used anywhere — the last mockup that reached for Menlo flattered the design by about 30% more ink than the device can put on the glass.

What ships — HOME and six groups, on both boards

HOME plus Device, Display, Sound, Pairing, Messages and Danger. The pager band became a back band of exactly the same height, so every group keeps PAGE_TOP 104 and not one page body had to be re-derived. Each HOME row carries its group's current value, so the common question — what brightness, how many Macs, is the host talking to me — is answered with no tap at all. Drawn here at board 2's geometry; board 1 draws the same surfaces from its own header, in a 222px page region against this board's 356px.

Every page lands between 14 and 174px of slack, and the loose ones are NAMED. Measured by the headless checker that renders all fifteen screens:

Danger's 174 and Messages' 80 are not padding and not an oversight: a page with enough air in it is a page whose constants are constrained by nothing, so each surplus has a name in the header (P2_AIR_BOT, P4_AIR_BOT, P1_AIR_BOT, PS_AIR_BOT, DEV_AIR_BOT, HOME_Y0_BOT) and settings-geom-check.mjs asserts the closing SUM rather than the individual numbers. The Pairing figure is for three Macs and the button; with all four slots filled it is 16, which is the case the geometry actually has to survive and the case where PAIR NEW MAC is correctly absent.
DEVICE is two slimmed cards over a six-line diagnostics block. The two live cards went 112 → 70 each, because each lost a line that was not about the question the card answers: the device name and the paired count moved to Pairing (a setting, beside the Macs it counts), and the SoC temperature became a diagnostics column. That paid for eleven facts — four host diagnostics, five About rows, the device's own name and the die temperature — which were previously a full card plus a whole About page, about 416px, for values nobody watches. Two facts do not reach the glass and that is written down rather than implied: the BT MAC's own line carries it, but the build TIME sits beside the date on the last line and the eleven facts fit eight two-column slots only because line DEV_DIAG_TEMP_LINE is deliberately solo.

That line is solo because it is the one whose COLOUR means something. A diagnostics line is one padded field with one colour, so a temperature sharing a line with the flush figure would have coloured the flush figure by temperature too. Giving it its own line is what let colorForDieTemp()'s warm/hot band come back — it had been left uncalled on both boards, so no warm/hot signal existed anywhere on the device.

Board 1 draws no diagnostics block at all and declares none of its constants (BOARD_DEVICE_DIAGNOSTICS), because that board has a CH340 console and these facts are a screen away. It carries CALIBRATE TOUCH under a SETUP caption instead.
DANGER holds exactly two controls, and both of them destroy state. It was called Actions and was a bag of verbs; MIC TEST left for Sound and CALIBRATE TOUCH left for board 1's Device group (board 2 does not offer it at all — the touch controller is factory-aligned inside the display IC, runCalibration() is not compiled there and RECAL is refused by name). What is left is RESET PAIRING and POWER OFF, so the name says what the page is for, which is the one thing standing between a finger and a destructive control.

The severity spine exists because that page was breaking this repo's own rule. Every action button was an identically shaped outlined slab differing only in stroke hue, on a device where session status gets a filled square, a hollow ring and a distinct mark precisely so that meaning is never carried by colour alone. A solid P2_SPINE_W (4px) bar carries severity as ink mass; the caption carries it as position; colour is the third cue rather than the only one. What keeps the bar off the corner arcs is its Y-inset, not its width. The greyscale claim is ARGUED, not measured — palette-check.mjs can test a colour pair and nothing in this repo tests a SHAPE.
PAIRING gained the two things it lacked. The live Mac rows moved here from the old page 0, where they were a second, differently-formatted copy of this same list; what the list gained with them is whether a remembered Mac is talking right now. And PAIR NEW MAC takes the list's next free slot, so its absence at four Macs is how the page says "full" — the same limit twice over, since the slot with no room on the screen is also the slot with no room in NVS. The ONLY tag rides beside the name rather than replacing anything, because selection here is the card's border plus a tag: two carriers, never hue alone.
SOUND is a real group, not a leftover. It carries output and input, because a mic test is a sound test — and it is the one action you run repeatedly, since MIC_GAIN is settled by watching MICMON while speaking rather than computed. TEST BEEP earns its place for the same reason: the ES8311's volume scale is linear in dB, so LOW/MED/HIGH are roughly 13dB apart and everything under ~40 is inaudible on this hardware — a word cannot tell you what a volume sounds like. There is no bar under VOLUME, deliberately: only BRIGHTNESS gets one, because it is the single continuous 0–100 setting and a bar under three named presets would be decoration.

The seven-group design — superseded by the amendment of 2026-09-12

This mock drew seven groups until board 1 was measured. Kept and marked rather than deleted, which is this repo's rule about descriptions that turned out to be wrong: a reader comparing the spec's body against its amendment can see both pictures instead of taking the difference on trust. Display, Sound and Pairing were identical in both designs, so only the three screens the amendment actually moved are drawn.

THE FIVE-GROUP SET WAS IMPOSSIBLE ON BOARD 1, and so was folding the destructive verbs into Pairing. Measured, from board_e32r28t.h: board 1's Macs page spends 216 of its 222px — the ANY MAC row at 82, four Mac rows at step 44 ending at 298, in a region of 80..302. RESET PAIRING needs caption 13 + SET_CAP_STEP 21 + P2_BTN_H 38 = 72px that do not exist. Abutting the rows saves 12; deleting the ANY MAC row saves 44 and deletes a real setting; both together still leave it 14 short; and capping the list at three slots breaks the four-Mac case, which is the only case the geometry has to survive. So the sixth group stayed, holding both destructive verbs, and it was renamed Danger — which is what it now is.

Three things that bought, none of them consolation prizes. The "destructive group LAST" rule is preserved rather than reversed, so this design no longer contradicts a written rule anywhere. Board 2's HOME stayed at the 58/10 six-row pitch. And Device regained the ~80px POWER OFF was occupying, which is exactly what the SoC temperature needed to get its own line and its colour band back.

The cost is written down too: board 1's HOME carries six rows at 42px, which is TAP_MIN + 2 — its rows sit near the fingertip floor rather than at the roomy five-row pitch. 42 is the only row height in the whole solution space with any margin over that floor, and the space was searched and enumerated (six (row, gap) pairs, 46 four-tuples) rather than claimed unique. Board 2's own six-row space has 281 (R, G) pairs, so the uniqueness this spec once asserted for it was never there either.

What shipped before the branch — for comparison

The pager cost 58px of every page and gave no overview. CONTENT_Y 46 to contentBottom() 460 is 414px; PAGER_H 54 plus its 4px gap is 14% of it, spent identically on all four pages purely to say where you are. There was no screen that showed what is set, the dots were not tappable, and STATUS to PAIRED MACS was three taps. Reaching any group is now exactly one tap, and zero for the questions HOME's summaries already answer. The honest cost is the adjacent case: moving between two groups is back-then-in, two taps, where the pager moved to a neighbour in one. That trade was taken knowingly.

A defect found while measuring this, and it is the reason this mock is bound to the header at all. board_es3c35p.h's ACTIONS note opened "THREE buttons, not four: BOARD_HAS_MIC is 0 here" — and line 18 of that same header was #define BOARD_HAS_MIC 1, set when the ES8311 capture path landed. The whole arithmetic chain went with the premise: it put the hint at y=302 with 148px clear, where the real values were 364 and 86px. Nothing could have caught it — every checker in this repo parses const int declarations and macros, and a comment is neither. It is worse than a wrong number, because it is the paragraph a reader consults BEFORE the constants and its arithmetic was internally consistent. That class was corrected ten times on this branch, the tenth inside the commit that swept fifteen sites for it.
